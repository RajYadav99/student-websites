The Rescue Mission

Ek nanhe gilahari ke bache ki bachaav aur naye jeevan ki kahani

1. Wo Pehla Din (The Discovery)

Ek shaam tez tufan aur baarish ke baad, humein garden mein ped ke neeche ek chota sa jeev mila. Wo bilkul bezubaan aur kaanp raha tha. Dhyan se dekhne par pata chala ki wo gilahari ka ek chota sa bacha tha, jo shayad tez hawa ke chalte apne ghosle se gir gaya tha. Uska sharir bilkul gulabi tha aur uski aankhein band thin.

2. Pehli Chunauti (First Aid & Warmth)

Gilahari ke bache ko sabse pehle garmi ki zaroorat hoti hai. Humne use turant ek naram kapde mein lapeta aur ek chhote se box mein safe rakha. Humne uski maa ka intezar kiya, lekin jab kaafi der tak koi nahi aaya, toh humne faisla kiya ki ab iski dekhbhal humein hi karni hogi. Use dropper ki madad se thoda sa gunguna paani aur ORS ka ghol diya gaya taaki dehydration na ho.

3. Har Do Ghante Ki Mehnat (Feeding Routine)

Shuruaat ke do hafte sabse mushkil the. Bache ko har 2-3 ghante mein special milk formula (Puppy Milk Replacer/KMR) dena padta tha, bilkul ek chhote bache ki tarah. Dhire-dhire uske sharir par baal (fur) aane lage aur uski peeth par gilahari ki wo sundar dhaariyan (stripes) dikhne lagin.

4. Aankhein Khulna Aur Pehli Udaan (Growing Up)

Lagbhag 4 hafte baad, wo khubsoorat pal aaya jab usne pehli baar apni chhoti-chhoti aankhein kholin! Ab wo aas-paas ki duniya ko samajhne laga tha. Humne use dheere-dheere solids jaise nuts, seb ke chhote tukde aur daliya khilana shuru kiya. Ab wo box se nikal kar kamre mein kood-faand karne laga tha.

5. Azadi Ki Ore (Ready for the Wild)

Kuch hi mahino mein, wo kamzor bacha ab ek chust aur chalak gilahari ban chuka tha, jiski ek ghani fluffy poonch thi. Halanki humein usse bahut lagav ho gaya tha, par hum jante the ki uska asli ghar prakriti (nature) mein hai. Humne use dheere-dheere bahar ki duniya se introduce karwaya aur azaad kar diya. Aaj bhi wo kabhi-kabhi hamari khidki par nuts khane zaroor aata hai!
